### **Research & Learn**

1. **What are the key responsibilities of a Scrum Master?**
   - Facilitate Scrum meetings (standups, sprint planning, retrospectives).
   - Help the team remove blockers and stay productive.
   - Ensure Agile principles are followed and drive continuous improvement.

2. **How does a Scrum Master facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, sprint planning, retrospectives)?**
   - **Standups**: Keep them short and focused on progress and blockers.
   - **Sprint Planning**: Ensure clear sprint goals and realistic task estimation.
   - **Retrospectives**: Encourage honest feedback and actionable improvements.

3. **What techniques help Scrum Masters remove blockers and improve team collaboration?**
   - Identify and address issues early.
   - Improve communication within the team.
   - Mediate conflicts and encourage teamwork.

4. **How do Scrum Masters support continuous improvement in Agile teams?**
   - Guide teams in retrospectives to implement real changes.
   - Monitor team progress and suggest improvements.
   - Foster an Agile mindset for long-term success.

### **Reflection**

1. **If a team member consistently misses standups, how should a Scrum Master address it?**
   - Ask why they are missing meetings and find a solution.
   - Explain the importance of standups for team alignment.
   - Offer an alternative update method if needed.

2. **How can a Scrum Master help resolve conflicts within the team?**
   - Act as a neutral mediator and encourage open discussion.
   - Focus on facts and data, not personal opinions.
   - Promote a respectful and solution-oriented culture.

3. **What are some strategies to improve sprint retrospectives and make them more effective?**
   - Have team members prepare feedback in advance.
   - Use different formats (e.g., Start-Stop-Continue) to keep it engaging.
   - Ensure follow-up actions are taken after the meeting.
