### Understanding Agile & Scrum at Focus Bear

1. **What are the Agile Manifesto principles, and how do they influence software development?**
   Agile values flexibility, collaboration, and delivering working software quickly. It helps teams focus on customer needs and adapt to changes efficiently.

2. **What is Scrum, and how does it structure work in Agile teams?**
   Scrum is a way to organize work in short cycles (sprints) with clear goals, roles, and regular check-ins to keep things on track.

3. **What are Scrum ceremonies?**
   - **Sprint Planning**: Decide what to work on in the next sprint.
   - **Daily Standups**: Quick meetings to check progress and discuss roadblocks.
   - **Sprint Review**: Show completed work to stakeholders.
   - **Sprint Retrospective**: Discuss what went well and what to improve for next time.

4. **What are the biggest benefits of Agile & Scrum for software teams?**
   - Faster delivery of useful software
   - Easier to adapt to changes
   - Better teamwork and communication

5. **How does Scrum help teams stay adaptable and improve continuously?**
   Regular reviews and retrospectives help teams adjust their work process and improve over time.

6. **What challenges do teams face when implementing Agile, and how can they overcome them?**
   - **Resistance to change** → Educate and involve the team in the process.
   - **Unclear priorities** → Keep the backlog organized and updated.
   - **Poor communication** → Encourage open discussions and regular check-ins.
