### **Research & Learn**  

1. **What are the key roles in Scrum, and how does Focus Bear’s team structure differ?**  
   - **Scrum Master**: Facilitates meetings, removes blockers, and ensures Agile practices are followed.  
   - **Developers**: Build and implement features.  
   - **QA (Quality Assurance)**: Test and ensure product quality.  
   - **UX Designers**: Focus on user experience and design.  
   - **Product Manager** (instead of Product Owner): Focuses on overall product strategy, customer needs, and team alignment.  
   - **Difference at Focus Bear**: A **Product Manager** leads product vision instead of a traditional **Product Owner**.  

2. **What is the role of a Product Manager in Agile, and how does it differ from a traditional Product Owner?**  
   - **Product Manager**: Defines product vision, ensures alignment with business goals, collaborates across teams.  
   - **Product Owner**: Manages backlog, works closely with developers, prioritizes tasks based on immediate project needs.  

3. **What responsibilities do Scrum Masters have in sprint planning, team facilitation, and continuous improvement?**  
   - **Sprint Planning**: Helps set sprint goals, ensures team capacity is balanced.  
   - **Team Facilitation**: Runs standups, removes blockers, promotes communication.  
   - **Continuous Improvement**: Leads retrospectives and optimizes team workflows.  

4. **What responsibilities do QA and UX designers have in an Agile environment?**  
   - **QA**: Test features, ensure stability, report bugs, automate testing where possible.  
   - **UX Designers**: Conduct user research, create wireframes, refine UI based on feedback.  

### **Reflection**  

1. **How do Scrum roles work together to ensure project success?**  
   - The **Scrum Master** keeps the team running smoothly.  
   - The **Product Manager** aligns the team with business needs.  
   - **Developers, QA, and UX** collaborate to build and refine the product.  

2. **How does a Product Manager's role impact sprint planning and backlog prioritization?**  
   - Ensures sprint goals align with product vision.  
   - Works with the team to prioritize tasks based on business value and user needs.  

3. **If a developer is struggling to complete a task, whose responsibility is it to help unblock them?**  
   - **Scrum Master**: Helps remove blockers and improve workflows.  
   - **Other developers**: Offer technical support and collaboration.  
   - **Product Manager**: Provides clarity on product requirements if needed.
