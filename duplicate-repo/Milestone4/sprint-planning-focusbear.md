# Sprint Planning Reflection: Focus Bear

## How the Team Picks Tasks

In the Sprint Planning meeting, the team first reviewed tasks from the backlog. They chose tasks based on priority and importance. Everyone discussed which tasks should be included, focusing clearly on the sprint goal.

## How the Team Estimates Tasks

The team used story points to estimate how much work each task required. Each person gave their estimate, and if team members disagreed, they briefly discussed the task until everyone reached agreement.

## Challenges Noticed

- Sometimes, team members underestimated how complicated tasks were.
- A few tasks were unclear, making it hard for the team to estimate accurately.

## Suggestions for Improvement

- Tasks should be made clearer before the meeting to save discussion time.
- Big or unclear tasks should be broken down into smaller, clearer ones.

## Final Thoughts

Overall, the Sprint Planning session at Focus Bear was efficient. The team set realistic goals and worked together well. However, clearer task descriptions would make the planning even better.
