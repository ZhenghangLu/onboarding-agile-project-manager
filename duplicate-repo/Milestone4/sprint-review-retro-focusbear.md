# Sprint Review & Retrospective Reflection: Focus Bear

## Sprint Review

### What Worked Well
- The team clearly showcased completed work, making it easy for stakeholders to see progress.
- Stakeholders gave direct and helpful feedback during presentations.

### Challenges
- Some stakeholders were hesitant to give constructive criticism openly.
- Occasionally, discussions became too detailed, taking more time than planned.

### Suggestions for Improvement
- Encourage stakeholders to prepare feedback beforehand.
- Clearly set time limits for discussions to keep meetings concise.

## Sprint Retrospective

### What Worked Well
- Team members openly shared their experiences and challenges.
- Used the Start/Stop/Continue format, making feedback clear and actionable.

### Challenges
- Sometimes team members avoided bringing up sensitive issues.
- Occasionally, action points from previous retrospectives were forgotten.

### Improvement Idea
- Clearly document action items and review them at the start of each retrospective.

## Final Thoughts
Focus Bear’s Sprint Reviews and Retrospectives effectively promoted transparency and continuous improvement. Addressing stakeholder feedback clarity and consistently tracking retrospective action items can further enhance these meetings.
