### Company Overview & Reflection Assignments

1. **Why do you think Focus Bear was created?**
   To help people with ADHD and ASD improve their productivity.

2. **What problem is Focus Bear solving?**
   It allows users to clear external distractions and focus on productivity work, while reminding users to take a break at the right time and allowing them to develop good habits.

3. **Why do you think this mission is important?**
   For people with ADHD and ASD, it is difficult to focus on productivity work. Focus Bear can help them focus on work to a certain extent. At the same time, it can provide users with good work habits to avoid neck pain and back pain.

4. **How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values ​​or interests?**
   I personally suffer from severe anxiety and depression, so it is difficult for me to focus on work for a long time. I appreciate the development of this use case, and I also hope that this application can be more comprehensive and practical to help people like me.

5. **Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?**
   Yes, I have been diagnosed with severe anxiety and depression, which makes it difficult for me to focus on work for a long time. I hope that using Focus Bear can improve my habits and allow me to work and live better.
