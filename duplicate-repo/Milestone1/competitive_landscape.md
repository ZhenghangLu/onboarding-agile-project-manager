### Competitive Landscape

1. **How is Focus Bear different from these apps?**
   Focus Bear is specifically focused on people with ADHD and ASD, while other apps focus more on blocking websites and software.

2. **If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over its competitors?**
   Focus Bear focuses on habit formation, such as reminding you to relax and exercise at appropriate times, rather than simply forcibly blocking some web pages.

3. **What other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't have?**
   For example, Cold Turkey and Freedom can block websites by entering a URL, or use `*` plus keywords to block related content.

4. **Based on your research, what is one aspect that you think Focus Bear could improve?**
   Add a forced blocking function similar to Cold Turkey to help users more completely block the use of certain URLs and apps.
