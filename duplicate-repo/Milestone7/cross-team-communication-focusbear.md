# Cross-Team Communication Reflection (Focus Bear)

## Importance of Good Communication

As a PM at Focus Bear, I’ve learned that clear communication between Developers, QAs, and PMs is essential for smooth projects. When everyone understands each other clearly, we ship better products and avoid unnecessary stress.

## Common Miscommunication Issues

Typical issues include:
- Unclear requirements or expectations from PMs.
- Developers and QAs disagreeing about the severity of bugs.
- Late or incomplete feedback causing confusion.

## Balancing Feature Development, Bugs, and Testing

We aim to:
- Clearly prioritize tasks during sprint planning.
- Allocate specific time each sprint for fixing bugs.
- Involve QA from the start to avoid surprises later on.

## Resolving Conflicts Between Teams

As a PM, I suggest Scrum Masters:
- Encourage open discussion about disagreements.
- Clarify roles clearly—who decides bug severity, feature priority, etc.
- Focus conversations on solving the issue, not blaming people.

## Keeping Information Flowing

To prevent bottlenecks:
- PMs should give clear, detailed requirements.
- Developers regularly update progress, and communicate openly about issues.
- QAs consistently share testing results and feedback quickly.

## Handling Disagreements on Bug Severity

If Developers and QAs disagree:
- Facilitate a quick, face-to-face conversation to clarify impact.
