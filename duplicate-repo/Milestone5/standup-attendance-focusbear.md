# Standup Attendance Reflection (Focus Bear)

## Why Standup Attendance Matters

At Focus Bear, daily standups help us keep everyone on the same page. Tracking attendance makes sure each team member stays involved. Regular attendance prevents miscommunication and keeps projects running smoothly.

## How We Use Google Sheets for Tracking

We use a simple Google Sheet to track who attends our daily standups. Each day we mark whether team members are "Present," "Late," or "Absent." This helps us quickly notice if someone is regularly missing or facing difficulties.

## Patterns We Noticed

From reviewing attendance:
- Most team members attend regularly, but a few occasionally miss due to personal reasons or timezone differences.
- If someone misses several days in a row, it usually signals they might need support or assistance.

## Team Member Feedback

After talking with the team:
- Members agreed attendance tracking helps them stay accountable.
- When someone misses repeatedly, it's easier for the team to reach out, offer help, and prevent small problems from growing.

## Improving Communication and Engagement

Tracking attendance improves our communication by quickly highlighting issues. If someone regularly misses standups, the Scrum Master should privately discuss the reasons—such as workload, timezones, or personal issues—and try adjusting the timing or approach to help them engage better.

## Making Standups More Engaging

- Keep standups short (under 15 minutes).
- Maintain a positive and relaxed environment.
- Let team members freely share their challenges instead of just daily updates.

## Conclusion

At Focus Bear, using Google Sheets to track standup attendance has improved our team's communication, accountability, and overall effectiveness. It’s a simple practice that has made our team stronger.
